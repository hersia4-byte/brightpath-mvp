AI Receptionist Service Agreement

This Service Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of (“Effective Date”) between (“Provider”) and (“Client”), located at .

1. Services

Provider will configure, deploy, and maintain an AI-powered virtual phone receptionist (the “Service”) for Client that will:

  • Answer inbound calls forwarded from Client's phone line per the forwarding option selected during intake;
  • Respond to caller questions using only information Client approves in the Client Intake Form;
  • Check availability and schedule appointments per Client's stated schedule;
  • Record structured messages and deliver call summaries to Client;
  • Transfer emergency calls, as defined by Client during intake, to Client's designated number.

Provider will review call transcripts and tune the Service during the first fourteen (14) days after go-live, and will implement Client-requested updates to approved information (hours, pricing, availability, policies) within two (2) business days of written request.

2. Trial Period

The first fourteen (14) days following go-live are a free trial. Client may cancel in writing at any point during the trial and owe nothing, including the setup fee. If Client does not cancel, billing begins automatically on day 15.

4. Term and Termination

This Agreement runs month-to-month from the Effective Date. Provider may terminate immediately if Client uses the Service for unlawful purposes. Upon termination, Provider will disable the Service, provide Client an export of its booking and message data on request within thirty (30) days, and then delete Client data per Section 8.

5. Client Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate, complete information in the Intake Form and keep it current; the Service states only what Client approves;
  • Configure call forwarding from Client's phone line (Provider will assist);
  • Promptly review call summaries and flag any concerns during the tuning period;
  • Comply with applicable call-recording notice and consent laws in Client's jurisdiction (see Section 7);
  • Not represent the Service as a live human to callers or in advertising.

6. Nature of the Service; AI Disclosure and Limitations

The Service is powered by artificial intelligence and identifies itself to callers as a virtual assistant. Client acknowledges that AI systems may occasionally mishear, misunderstand, or respond imperfectly. The Service is designed to take a message rather than guess when it is uncertain, and all calls are logged and transcribed for review. The Service is not an emergency service and does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice; emergency calls are transferred per Client's intake instructions. Provider does not guarantee any particular business outcome, including appointment or revenue volume.

7. Call Recording and Consent

Calls handled by the Service are recorded and transcribed to provide call summaries and quality review. Where required by law, the Service will state that calls may be recorded. Client is responsible for confirming the notice and consent requirements that apply to Client's business and jurisdiction, including two-party-consent states.

8. Data and Privacy

  • Client owns its business information and all caller data collected on its behalf; Provider owns the underlying system, prompts, configurations, and software;
  • Provider processes call audio, transcripts, bookings, and messages solely to deliver the Service, using third-party processors (telephony, speech recognition, language-model, and hosting providers) under their respective terms;
  • Provider will not sell caller data or use it to market to callers;
  • Provider will maintain reasonable safeguards, and will delete Client and caller data within sixty (60) days of termination, except records required for legal or accounting purposes;
  • If Client's industry imposes specific data obligations (e.g., health information), the parties will document required additional terms in writing before go-live.

9. Service Levels

Provider will operate the Service with commercially reasonable skill and care. The Service depends on third-party platforms (telephony carriers, AI model providers, hosting); Provider does not warrant uninterrupted operation but will restore outages promptly and notify Client of any outage exceeding four (4) business hours. If the Service is unavailable for more than twenty-four (24) consecutive hours due to causes within Provider's control, Client receives a pro-rata credit for the affected period on request.
