# Vertical Pack — Home Services (Plumbing, HVAC, Electrical, Roofing…)

## Why this niche

A missed call IS a missed job — homeowners with a burst pipe call down the
Google list until a human (or convincing agent) answers. Average ticket
$300–$800; emergency jobs and installs run $5k–$15k. Owners are in crawlspaces
all day and know exactly how many calls they miss. Easiest niche to prove ROI:
count booked jobs.

## Positioning line

> "When you're under a sink, who answers your phone? Because the homeowner
> calling right now is already dialing your competitor."

## Pricing anchor

$297 / **$497** / $797 (solo / small crew / dispatch-heavy). Justify against
one recovered job per month — that's the whole subscription paid.

## `{{VERTICAL_KNOWLEDGE_BLOCK}}` — extra intake questions

- Services offered and NOT offered (crucial — what jobs do they decline?)
- Service area by city/zip; travel fee policy
- Dispatch/trip fee and whether it applies to estimates
- Emergency availability: 24/7? surcharge? which trades?
- Financing options for big-ticket installs
- Licensing/insurance/bond numbers to state if asked (builds instant trust)

## `{{VERTICAL_PRIVACY_RULE}}`

> Default rule is enough (no other customers' info). Additionally: never quote
> a firm price for work sight-unseen — give the listed range or dispatch fee
> and book the estimate.

## Niche-specific notes

- **Urgency triage is the demo-wow:** emergency → immediate transfer to the
  on-call tech; urgent → book same/next day and mark the message urgent;
  routine → next open slot. Script exactly this in the demo call.
- After-hours mode is the natural entry product: they keep answering days,
  the agent catches the 7pm water-heater calls they currently lose.
- Great expansion: outbound "your technician is 20 minutes out" calls, and
  review-request texts after completed jobs (+$100–150/mo each).
