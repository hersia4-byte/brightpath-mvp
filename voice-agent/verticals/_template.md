# Vertical Pack — {{Industry Name}}

Copy this file when a customer from a new industry asks. Fill every section —
it takes about 30 minutes of research (their industry's forums, 3 competitor
websites, one call with the prospect). Everything else in the package stays
untouched.

## Why this niche

What does a missed call cost them, in dollars? Who answers the phone today, and
why do they miss calls? Who is the decision maker and how reachable are they?

## Positioning line

One sentence that proves you understand their specific pain. Best formula:
describe the exact moment they lose money to a missed call.

## Pricing anchor

Low / **recommended** / high tier. Justify against the dollar value of ONE
recovered customer, never against the cost of answering phones.

## Core variable suggestions

| Variable | Suggested value |
|---|---|
| `AGENT_NAME` | (friendly first name) |
| `APPOINTMENT_TYPE` | (what gets booked: consult, estimate, viewing, demo…) |
| `APPOINTMENT_VERB` | (how a caller phrases it) |

| `APPOINTMENT_OFFER_LINE` | (the natural one-liner that moves a caller to book) |
| `MESSAGE_CATEGORIES` | (2–4 message types their phone actually gets) |
| `EMERGENCY_EXAMPLES` | (what must transfer to a human immediately) |

## `{{VERTICAL_KNOWLEDGE_BLOCK}}` — extra intake questions

The 5–10 industry-specific facts the agent must know to answer this industry's
top phone questions. Source them from the client's website and the intake call.

## `{{VERTICAL_PRIVACY_RULE}}`

Any industry rule about what must never be shared (patient info, tenant info,
children, financial details). If none beyond the default, say "default rule."

## Niche-specific notes

Regulatory landmines, the demo moment that wows this industry, objections
unique to them, and expansion products they'd pay extra for.
