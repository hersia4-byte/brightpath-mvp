# Vertical Pack — Realtors & Property Managers

## Why this niche

Speed-to-lead is everything: a buyer/renter who doesn't reach someone in 5
minutes calls the next listing. Agents are in showings all day and physically
cannot answer. Property managers drown in maintenance calls. This was the exact
niche in the "$397 MRR first sale" testimonial — it closes fast because the
buyer already knows missed calls cost commissions.

## Positioning line

> "How many calls did you miss during showings this week? Each one was a lead
> that called the next agent."

## Pricing anchor

$297 / **$397–$597** / $997 (solo agent / team or small PM / PM with 100+ doors).
Justify against one commission (~$8–12k) or one signed lease.

## `{{VERTICAL_KNOWLEDGE_BLOCK}}` — extra intake questions

- Active listings (address, beds/baths, price, 1-line highlights) — or a rule:
  "for listing details, collect which property and book a showing"
- Rental criteria: income multiple, credit threshold, pet policy, deposits
- Application process and fee
- For PMs: maintenance request process, emergency vs. routine definition,
  after-hours vendor to transfer emergencies to

## `{{VERTICAL_PRIVACY_RULE}}`

> Never discuss a tenant's, buyer's, or seller's personal or financial details
> with anyone. Never state why a property is being sold or a tenant is leaving.
> Fair housing: answer questions about the PROPERTY, never about neighborhood
> demographics or "what kind of people" live anywhere — decline and offer facts
> about the property itself.

## Niche-specific notes

- Maintenance triage is the killer feature for PMs: emergency → transfer to
  on-call number; routine → structured message with unit, issue, access
  permission. Demo exactly that.
- Solo realtors buy fastest but churn; property managers are slower to close
  and stick for years. Land both, prioritize PMs for stability.
