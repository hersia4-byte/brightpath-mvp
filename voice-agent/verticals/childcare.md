# Vertical Pack — Childcare / Daycare Centers

## Why this niche

One enrollment is worth $10,000–$20,000/year. Front desk can't answer during
pickup/drop-off chaos; a parent who hits voicemail calls the next center on the
list. Owner usually answers the phone at small centers — easy to reach the
decision maker.

## Positioning line

> "I called your center yesterday at 5:20pm and got voicemail. So did every
> parent who called."

## Pricing anchor

$297 / **$497** / $997 (after-hours / full reception / multi-site).
Justify against one enrollment's value, not against phone-answering.

## Core variable suggestions

| Variable | Suggested value |
|---|---|
| `AGENT_NAME` | Sunny |
| `APPOINTMENT_TYPE` | facility tour |
| `APPOINTMENT_VERB` | book a tour |
| `APPOINTMENT_EXTRA_FIELDS` | child's age; program of interest |
| `APPOINTMENT_OFFER_LINE` | "The best way to get a feel for us is to visit — can I set you up with a quick tour?" |
| `MESSAGE_CATEGORIES` | absence, late_pickup, callback |
| `EMERGENCY_EXAMPLES` | injured or sick child in care, anything about immediate child safety |

## `{{VERTICAL_KNOWLEDGE_BLOCK}}` — extra intake questions

- Ages served (youngest–oldest)
- Programs/rooms, 1 line each (infants, toddlers, pre-K, after-school)
- Which rooms have openings right now (update monthly!)
- Waitlist policy and fee
- Staff:child ratios by room
- Meals policy + allergy accommodation statement
- Sick policy (the 1-paragraph parent version)
- Curriculum/philosophy blurb

## `{{VERTICAL_PRIVACY_RULE}}` (non-negotiable)

> If the caller asks about a specific child's day, health, or whereabouts:
> do not share ANY information about any child, ever. Take a message and say a
> staff member will call back promptly.

## Objections specific to this niche

- **"Parents will hate a robot."** → It only takes calls that would hit
  voicemail; anything urgent transfers to staff instantly.
- **Licensing worry** → The agent takes routine messages and books tours; it
  doesn't collect children's records or replace required staff communications.
